## What We're Testing

**Core Hypothesis:** Real curriculum (20+ exercises per lesson with explanations) is better than auto-generated.

**Measure:** User rating of 1–5

**Threshold:** ≥4/5 by 3+ testers = hypothesis validated

---

## Potential Outcomes & Next Steps

### Outcome A: Curriculum ≥4/5 ✅
**Data:** 3+ testers rate curriculum 4 or 5

**Interpretation:** Users value real curriculum. Continue building.

**Phase 2 Plan:**
- Build 2–3 more lessons (V2 Advanced, Verb System, Real-world Listening)
- Add progress tracking & analytics
- Expand to 20–50 testers (public beta)
- 4-week sprint

### Outcome B: Curriculum <3/5 ❌
**Data:** Most testers rate curriculum 1–3

**Interpretation:** Real curriculum may not be worth the effort.

**Phase 2 Plan:**
- Simplify to 5–10 template exercises per module
- Focus on assessment + roadmap quality
- Defer curriculum to Phase 3
- 2-week reassessment

### Outcome C: Mixed (3–4/5) ?
**Data:** Half rate high, half rate low

**Interpretation:** Something about the curriculum resonates with some users, not others.

**Phase 2 Plan:**
- Investigate: What's different about high raters?
- Refine lessons based on specific feedback
- Test again with small cohort
- Then expand or pivot
